# Standard Operating Procedure (SOP): LabourConnect Platform

## 1. Purpose
The purpose of this document is to define the standard operating procedures for the LabourConnect platform, ensuring a safe, efficient, and reliable connection between daily labourers and service seekers.

## 2. Scope
This SOP applies to all platform users (Workers and Customers) and administrative staff.

---

## 3. User Onboarding & Registration

### 3.1 Worker Registration
1.  **Application:** Worker downloads the app and enters their mobile number for OTP verification.
2.  **Profile Setup:** Worker provides name, primary skill (e.g., Plumber, Mason, Electrician), and work location.
3.  **Aadhaar/ID Verification:**
    *   Worker uploads a photo of their Aadhaar card or Government ID.
    *   System performs OCR and matches details with a live "selfie" to prevent fraud.
    *   **Verification Status:** Profile remains "Pending" until an admin or automated third-party API validates the ID.
4.  **Language Selection:** Worker chooses their preferred Indian language (Hindi, Marathi, Tamil, etc.).

### 3.2 Customer Registration
1.  Customer registers via mobile OTP.
2.  Customer grants GPS permissions for location-based searching.

---

## 4. Search and Booking Process

### 4.1 Finding a Worker
1.  Customer uses the search bar or category icons to select a skill.
2.  Platform uses GPS to filter workers within a 5-10km radius.
3.  Customer views worker profiles including:
    *   Verification badge.
    *   Average rating and review history.
    *   Daily/Hourly rate.

### 4.2 Booking Workflow
1.  **Instant Booking:** For immediate needs, the customer clicks "Book Now." The worker has 120 seconds to accept the request.
2.  **Scheduling:** Customer selects a future date and time slot.
3.  **Confirmation:** Once accepted, both parties receive contact details and a secure 4-digit "Job Start Code."

---

## 5. Job Execution & Safety

### 5.1 Starting the Job
1.  Worker arrives at the GPS location.
2.  Customer provides the "Job Start Code" to the worker.
3.  Worker enters the code in the app to officially begin the session.

### 5.2 SOS Protocol (Worker & Customer Safety)
1.  An **SOS Button** is visible on the active job screen for both parties.
2.  **Activation:** Pressing the button immediately:
    *   Sends a high-priority alert to the nearest police station/emergency services.
    *   Notifies the platform’s 24/7 Safety Team.
    *   Records audio from the device for evidence.
    *   Shares real-time GPS coordinates with pre-saved emergency contacts.

---

## 6. Payments and Earnings

### 6.1 Payment Modes
1.  **Online:** Customer pays via UPI, Card, or Wallet within the app.
2.  **Cash:** If selected, the worker marks the payment as received in the app.
3.  **Commission:** The platform deducts a nominal convenience fee from online payments.

### 6.2 Earning Tracking
1.  Workers can view a "Wallet" section.
2.  Provides a breakdown of daily, weekly, and monthly earnings.
3.  Workers can request a payout to their linked bank account (processed within 24 hours).

---

## 7. Ratings and Reviews

1.  **Post-Job Prompt:** Once the worker marks the job as "Completed," both parties are prompted to rate each other (1-5 stars).
2.  **Feedback:** Customers can leave specific comments on quality, punctuality, and behavior.
3.  **Moderation:** Reviews containing profanity or personal contact information are automatically flagged for removal.

---

## 8. Administrative Oversight

1.  **Dispute Resolution:** If a customer or worker reports an issue (e.g., job not finished, overcharging), an admin agent reviews the GPS logs and chat history.
2.  **Ban Policy:** Users with a rating consistently below 3.0 or those involved in safety violations are permanently banned.
